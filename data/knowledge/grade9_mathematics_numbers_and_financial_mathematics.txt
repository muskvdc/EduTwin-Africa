LEARNING OBJECTIVES:
- Explain the key ideas of Numbers and financial mathematics using Grade 9 terminology.

TEACHING APPROACH:
- Start from prior knowledge and move from simple examples to more complex applications.
- Use South African examples where they naturally fit, without inventing unsupported facts.
- Adapt explanations to the learner's demonstrated understanding.
- For quantitative subjects, show working, units and checks for reasonableness.
- For humanities and languages, distinguish evidence from opinion and support interpretations.
- For practical and creative subjects, include planning, safe practice, creation/performance, evaluation and reflection where relevant.

ASSESSMENT FOCUS:

COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS:
- Memorising a definition is not the same as understanding or applying a concept.
- A rule or formula must only be used when its conditions are satisfied.
- An unsupported answer is weaker than one supported by appropriate working, evidence or reasoning.
